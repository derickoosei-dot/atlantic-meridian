How it works

  1. Camera and microphone are required. You can’t start without a working camera.
  2. Recording starts automatically the moment each question appears, including the thinking time.
  3. After the thinking time, the answer timer starts. You can finish early, but you can’t pause.
  4. One attempt only. There are no retakes, no re-recording, and no going back to an earlier question.
  5. Refreshing, closing this page, or opening the link in another tab or device ends the question that is on screen. It cannot be retaken.
  6. Please don’t switch tabs or windows during the interview. That activity is noted for the reviewer.

Before you begin

  • A quiet, well-lit place where your face is clearly visible
  • A computer or phone with a camera and microphone, on a stable connection
  • A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
  • Set aside without interruptions
