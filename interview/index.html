How it works

  1. Camera and microphone are required. You can’t start without a working camera.
  2. Room check first (recorded). Right after you click Start, you’ll slowly turn your camera around the whole room and your desk for about 20 seconds, to show there are no extra screens, notes, phones, or other people. Then the first question appears.
  3. One screen only. No second monitor, tablet, or phone in use, no notes, and no one else helping you. Keep your face in view the whole time.
  4. Recording starts the moment you click Start and runs continuously until you finish, including the moments between questions.
  5. Each question appears on screen while you are already recording. There is no separate preparation time: think out loud and answer right away.
  6. Each answer has a timer. Click Next question when you are done. When time runs out, the next question appears automatically shortly after.
  7. One attempt only. There are no retakes, no pausing, no re-recording, and no going back to an earlier question.
  8. Refreshing, closing this page, or opening the link in another tab or device ends the question that is on screen. It cannot be retaken.
  9. Your spoken answers are transcribed automatically to help the reviewer. Please don’t switch tabs or windows; that activity is noted.

Before you begin

  • A quiet, well-lit place where your face is clearly visible, alone in the room
  • Only one screen: turn off or put away other monitors, tablets, and phones
  • A camera you can turn or tilt (or a laptop/phone you can pick up) for the short room check
  • A computer or phone with a camera and microphone, on a stable connection
  • A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari, opened directly (not inside the Mail, Gmail, LinkedIn, or Indeed app)
  • Set aside without interruptions
