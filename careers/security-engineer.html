About us

Atlantic Meridian LLC is a Henderson, Nevada cybersecurity and AI consulting firm. We are building a bench of qualified professionals to name in proposals for upcoming public and private sector bids.

Duties

  • Design, deploy, and harden security controls (firewalls, EDR, IAM, MFA, logging)

  • Automate configuration baselines (CIS, DISA STIG)

Required qualifications

  • At least one of: CISSP, CCSP, GIAC GCLD or GSEC, AWS or Azure security specialty, CompTIA SecurityX (formerly CASP+)
