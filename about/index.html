About

Atlantic Meridian LLC is a minority-owned Henderson, Nevada small business. We help federal agencies, state and local governments, nonprofits, healthcare and education organizations, and commercial companies assess risk, meet compliance requirements, and strengthen security. Work is principal-led, scoped in writing, and available directly or as a subcontractor to primes.

What we do

Every engagement has defined deliverables in writing. Assessments are usually fixed fee. Longer authorization, compliance, or operations work can be scoped as fixed price or time and materials.

We support RMF and ATO work, NIST CSF 2.0, HIPAA, SOC 2, and CMMC readiness, plus IAM, PAM, cloud security, and AI governance aligned to the NIST AI RMF.
