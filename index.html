Core capabilities

Every engagement has defined deliverables in writing. Assessments are usually fixed fee. Longer authorization, compliance, or operations work can be scoped as fixed price or time and materials.

01 · Authorization

RMF / ATO support

Categorization, control selection, SSP authorship, security control assessment support, POA&M management, continuous monitoring, and ATO package readiness aligned to NIST SP 800-37, 800-53, and 800-171.
02 · Compliance

Risk and compliance assessments

NIST CSF 2.0 gap assessments, HIPAA Security Rule risk analysis, SOC 2 readiness, and CMMC / NIST 800-171 readiness for government, healthcare, education, nonprofit, and commercial organizations.
03 · Operations

SOC, EDR & detection

Detection engineering, alert triage, and SIEM/SOAR and EDR tuning, with automation that reduces noise for analysts.
04 · Identity

IAM, PAM & Zero Trust

Privileged access design, identity governance, MFA/SSO patterns, and least-privilege programs built to hold up in an audit.
05 · Cloud

Cloud & hybrid security

AWS, Azure, and hybrid security baselines, configuration reviews, and compliance-minded architecture.
06 · AI security

AI governance & AI-enabled security

AI governance aligned to the NIST AI RMF, plus practical AI-assisted triage and security workflow automation.
